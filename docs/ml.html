Train the ROI model from zero

The real scanner trains a small gradient-boosted model to predict the realised ROI of a trade-up, then uses it only as a tiebreaker next to the formula. This page runs the same idea in your browser on synthetic data. Press Train and the error falls, round by round, as boosting rounds are added. Nothing is pre-trained. The model starts from the mean and learns from there.

Reading it: the model beats the formula once the validation line drops below the dashed baseline. It should settle a little above the label-noise floor. Pushing below that would mean it is memorising noise. Raising the noise slider lifts the achievable floor with it. Raising the learning rate makes it converge faster, but it can wobble. In the real tool the model never overrides the formula. It only breaks ties between contracts the formula rates as close.
